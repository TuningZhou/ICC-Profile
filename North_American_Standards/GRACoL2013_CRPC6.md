# GRACoL 2013 CRPC6 / GRACoL 2013 CRPC6

## 概述 / Overview

**中文：** GRACoL 2013 CRPC6 是 IDEAlliance 更新的 GRACoL 配置文件，采用 M1 测量条件，TAC 降至 300%，适用于现代涂布纸印刷。

**English:** GRACoL 2013 CRPC6 is the updated GRACoL profile from IDEAlliance with M1 measurement and reduced 300% TAC for modern coated paper printing.
