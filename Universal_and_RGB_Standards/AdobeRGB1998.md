# Adobe RGB (1998) / Adobe RGB (1998)

## 概述 / Overview

**中文：** Adobe RGB (1998) 是 Adobe 定义的广色域 RGB 色彩空间，色域覆盖约 50% 的 CIE Lab 可见色，显著大于 sRGB。广泛用于专业摄影、印前处理和高端显示。

**English:** Adobe RGB (1998) is a wide-gamut RGB color space defined by Adobe, covering approximately 50% of CIE Lab visible colors, significantly larger than sRGB. Widely used in professional photography, prepress, and high-end displays.
