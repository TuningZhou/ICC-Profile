# Display P3 / Display P3

## 概述 / Overview

**中文：** Display P3 是基于 DCI-P3 数字电影标准的显示器色彩空间，由 Apple 推广用于 iPhone、iPad、Mac 等设备。色域比 sRGB 大约 25%，支持更鲜艳的红色和绿色。

**English:** Display P3 is a display color space based on the DCI-P3 digital cinema standard, popularized by Apple for iPhone, iPad, and Mac devices. Its gamut is ~25% larger than sRGB with more vivid reds and greens.
