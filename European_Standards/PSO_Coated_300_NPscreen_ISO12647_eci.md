# PSO Coated 300% NPscreen (ECI) / PSO 涂布纸 300% 调频加网（ECI）

## 概述 / Overview

**中文：** PSO Coated 300% NPscreen 专为调频（FM/随机）加网工艺设计，基于 FOGRA39 数据，总墨量降至 300%，适用于涂布纸上的非周期性加网印刷。

**English:** PSO Coated 300% NPscreen is designed for FM (stochastic/non-periodic) screening on coated paper, based on FOGRA39 data with TAC reduced to 300%.
