# ISO Uncoated Yellowish (ECI) / ISO 非涂布微黄纸

## 概述 / Overview

**中文：** ISO Uncoated Yellowish 适用于带有微黄色调的非涂布胶版纸，基于 FOGRA30 特性数据。常用于书籍内页、公文等微黄纸印刷。

**English:** ISO Uncoated Yellowish is designed for yellowish-toned uncoated offset paper, based on FOGRA30 characterization data. Commonly used for book interiors and stationery on yellowish stock.

## 适用场景 / Typical Applications

**中文：**
- 书籍内页印刷
- 公文与表格印刷
- 微黄纸张胶印

**English:**
- Book interior printing
- Stationery & form printing
- Offset on yellowish paper

## 注意事项 / Notes

**中文：** 专用于微黄色纸张，白色非涂布纸请使用 PSO_Uncoated 系列。

**English:** Specifically for yellowish paper; for white uncoated, use PSO_Uncoated profiles.
