# PSO MFC Paper (ECI) / PSO 机械涂布纸（ECI）

## 概述 / Overview

**中文：** PSO MFC Paper 适用于机械涂布纸上的轮转胶印，基于 FOGRA46 特性数据。MFC 纸介于涂布纸和非涂布纸之间。

**English:** PSO MFC Paper targets web offset on machine finished coated paper, based on FOGRA46 characterization data. MFC paper falls between coated and uncoated quality.

**English:**

**English:** Lower TAC (280%), suitable for medium-quality paper.
