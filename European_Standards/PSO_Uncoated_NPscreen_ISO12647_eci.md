# PSO Uncoated NPscreen (ECI) / PSO 非涂布纸调频加网（ECI）

## 概述 / Overview

**中文：** PSO Uncoated NPscreen 专为非涂布纸上的调频（FM/随机）加网工艺设计，基于 FOGRA47 数据。

**English:** PSO Uncoated NPscreen is designed for FM screening on uncoated paper, based on FOGRA47 data.
