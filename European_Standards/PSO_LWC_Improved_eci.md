# PSO LWC Improved (ECI) / PSO 改良型轻涂纸（ECI）

## 概述 / Overview

**中文：** PSO LWC Improved 适用于改良型轻量涂布纸上的热固型轮转胶印，基于 FOGRA45 特性数据。LWC Improved 纸张品质优于标准 LWC。

**English:** PSO LWC Improved targets heatset web offset on improved LWC paper, based on FOGRA45 characterization data. Improved LWC offers better quality than standard LWC.
