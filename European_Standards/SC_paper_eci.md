# SC Paper (ECI) / SC 超级压光纸（ECI）

## 概述 / Overview

**中文：** SC Paper 适用于超级压光纸上的轮转胶印，基于 FOGRA48 特性数据。SC 纸常用于大众杂志和广告印刷。

**English:** SC Paper targets web offset on super-calendered paper, based on FOGRA48 characterization data. SC paper is commonly used for mass-market magazines and advertising.

## 注意事项 / Notes

**English:** SC paper falls between newsprint and LWC in quality.
